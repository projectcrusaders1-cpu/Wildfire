About Project Crusaders

Helping communities feel more prepared.

Wildfire Watch brings wildfire awareness and practical preparedness guidance together in one clear, easy-to-use place. Explore wildfire basics, prevention ideas, and practical steps to help households prepare.

Explore the safety guide

This educational project does not provide live fire data or emergency alerts. Always follow official local instructions.

Awareness today. Preparedness for tomorrow.

What guides us

Our aim, purpose, and people

01

Our Aim

To make wildfire awareness easier to access and help people feel more confident about preparing for fire-related emergencies.

02

Our Purpose

Wildfire Watch shares a risk overview and practical safety information to encourage early planning, informed decisions, and safer communities.
