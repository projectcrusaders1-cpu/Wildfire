Wildfire risk by landscape

Wildfires affect different landscapes in different ways. Fuel, weather, terrain, and nearby communities all shape how a fire can spread and what may be at risk.

LANDSCAPES AT RISK

How wildfires affect different areas

Certain landscapes are more vulnerable because they have dry fuel, long periods of heat, and frequent human access.

Forest areas

Typical impact: Large forest fires can burn thousands of hectares in a short period, destroy trees, damage wildlife habitats, and threaten nearby communities.

Damage level: Very high — often 1,000 to 100,000+ hectares in severe seasons.

Root cause: High fuel load, heat, dry winds, and sometimes careless burning or sparks from equipment.

Grasslands and savannas

Typical impact: Fast-moving fires can spread quickly across open land, especially in windy conditions.

Damage level: Moderate to high — often tens to thousands of hectares can burn quickly.

Root cause: Dry grasses, agricultural residue, burning activities, and vehicle or equipment sparks.

Wildland-urban interface

Typical impact: Houses, roads, farms, and community services are exposed when settlements are close to forests or grass areas.

Damage level: Often the most expensive — many homes, farms, and roads can be damaged at once.

Root cause: Combined fire risk from dry vegetation, debris, electrical faults, and poor defensible space around homes.

Peat, farmland, and dry land

Typical impact: These fires can burn under the surface and keep smoldering for weeks.

Damage level: Severe — long-term soil, air-quality, and crop damage can continue for weeks or months.

Root cause: Drought, drained land, underground fuel, and sometimes illegal or careless burning operations.

REAL-LIFE RISKS

Reduce risk and stay prepared

When wildfire risk rises, the goal is to reduce ignition, protect people, and create safe routes and communication before the danger gets worse.

1. Watch for ignition sources

Never leave campfires, fireworks, grills, or equipment unattended. Sparks from vehicles, power tools, and discarded cigarettes can trigger fires in dry material.

2. Create defensible space

Keep grass short, remove dry leaves around buildings, and avoid stacking flammable material near walls or sheds.

3. Prepare evacuation routes

Know at least two exit routes, where to meet family members, and how to leave quickly if authorities issue an order to evacuate.

4. Reduce smoke exposure

Stay indoors when air quality becomes poor, seal windows if instructed, and use cleaner indoor air when possible. Keep N95 masks if available and fitted properly.

5. Report early

If you spot smoke or fire early, report it immediately. Early detection and fast alerts can prevent a small ignition from becoming a major disaster.

6. Help vulnerable people

Elderly people, children, people with disabilities, and pets need a plan, transport, medicines, and clear communication before an emergency begins.

EARLY WARNING · PREPAREDNESS

Where is early warning? And preparedness tips for before disaster? Everyone

Early warning is most effective when it reaches everyone: residents, workers, schools, elders, and small businesses. Preparedness must happen before a fire starts.

Where to find early warning

  • Local government emergency alerts, SMS, sirens, radio, and social media channels.
  • Weather and wildfire monitoring apps that are linked to official local authority updates.
  • Community leaders, local fire departments, schools, and neighborhood groups.
  • Authorities may issue evacuation orders, fire danger ratings, and smoke warnings depending on local conditions.

Preparedness tips for everyone

  • Prepare a go-bag with medicines, chargers, documents, water, food, and basic hygiene items.
  • Know your evacuation route, alternate route, and designated meeting point.
  • Practice the plan with your household, especially with children, elders, and pets.
  • Keep fuel, tools, and vehicles ready in case roads need to be used quickly.
  • Clear dry grass, leaves, and flammable material around homes and keep emergency contacts visible.
